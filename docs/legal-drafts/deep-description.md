# Deep research: plain description (for Anthropic support and the lawyer)

FirstLine is a B2B tool that helps a business write the first lines of its own cold emails from public facts about the company it's writing to. "Deep research" is an optional, per-campaign add-on that is built but switched off until we've had legal advice and Anthropic's confirmation. When it's on, for each contact in the customer's list, FirstLine runs one web search for the contact's name together with their company and reads at most two public pages that name both, never LinkedIn, social media or people-search sites. Claude (Haiku) then checks the pages are about that same person at that company and, only if it's sure, picks at most two facts about the person's own professional work for that company: an article they wrote, a talk or podcast they gave, or company news naming them. Code drops anything about family, health, age, home, religion or politics, and if there's no confident match nothing is used and the customer isn't charged. The facts are used only to write the opening line of that one email to that person (for example "your talk on refill packaging at Packaging Days"), with the source shown to our customer in a preview; there is no person profile, summary or search screen anywhere in the product. The facts are stored only with that lead in that customer's workspace, never shared with other customers or reused, and deleted with the lead data 30 days after the campaign ends; contacts on the customer's do-not-contact list are never searched.
